# Hardware Components

## Components Used

| No. | Component | Purpose |
|----:|-----------|---------|
| 1 | Wooden Supporting Structure | Supports the reflector assembly |
| 2 | Curved Metal Sheet | Provides the structural parabolic surface |
| 3 | Reflective Sheet | Reflects and concentrates solar radiation |
| 4 | Copper Pipe | Acts as the heat-absorbing water receiver |
| 5 | Pipe Support Brackets | Holds the copper pipe in position |
| 6 | Water Inlet Connection | Allows water to enter the system |
| 7 | Water Outlet Connection | Allows heated water to leave the system |
| 8 | Fasteners | Joins mechanical components |
| 9 | Adhesive/Fixing Material | Secures components |
| 10 | Base/Support Arrangement | Provides overall structural support |

## Copper Pipe

The copper pipe is positioned at the focal region of the parabolic reflector.

Copper is used because of its high thermal conductivity and suitability for transferring heat to the water flowing through the pipe.

The focal position used in the project is approximately:

**73 mm above the vertex**

## Reflective Sheet

The reflective sheet is mounted on the curved reflector surface.

Its purpose is to reflect incoming solar radiation toward the focal region.

## Wooden Structure

The wooden structure supports the curved metal sheet and maintains the required parabolic shape.

## Water Connections

The inlet and outlet connections allow water to flow through the heated copper pipe.

All connections should be checked for leakage before testing.
