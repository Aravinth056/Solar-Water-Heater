# Safety and Maintenance

## ⚠️ Safety Precautions

The following precautions should be followed while operating the **Parabolic Solar Water Heater Using Copper Pipe**.

### 1. Avoid Direct Exposure to the Focal Region

Do not look directly at the focal region of the parabolic reflector because concentrated sunlight can cause eye injury.

### 2. Avoid Contact with Hot Surfaces

The copper pipe and other metal parts can become hot during operation.

Use heat-resistant gloves when handling the system.

### 3. Keep Combustible Materials Away

Keep flammable and combustible materials away from the concentrated solar radiation and heated components.

### 4. Ensure Structural Stability

Make sure the reflector, copper pipe, supports, and base are stable before and during operation.

### 5. Check Water Connections

Check the inlet and outlet connections before operation to ensure that there are no water leaks.

### 6. Do Not Leave the System Unattended

The solar water heater should not be left unattended under strong sunlight.

Keep children and unauthorized persons away from the operating system.

## 🔧 Maintenance

### Reflective Surface

- Clean the reflective surface regularly.
- Remove dust and dirt.
- Check for scratches, wrinkles, and damaged areas.
- Replace damaged reflective material when required.

### Wooden Structure

- Inspect the wooden supports regularly.
- Check for cracks, loosening, or damage.
- Ensure that the structure maintains the required shape.

### Metal Sheet

- Inspect the curved metal sheet for deformation.
- Check for rust or corrosion.
- Ensure that the parabolic shape is maintained.

### Copper Pipe

- Check the pipe for leakage.
- Inspect for corrosion or blockage.
- Ensure that the pipe remains correctly positioned near the focal region.

### Water Connections

- Check the inlet and outlet connections.
- Inspect for leakage.
- Ensure proper water flow through the copper pipe.

### Support and Fasteners

- Check brackets and fasteners regularly.
- Tighten loose connections.
- Ensure that the reflector and copper pipe remain securely supported.
