# Working Principle

## 📌 Overview

The **Parabolic Solar Water Heater Using Copper Pipe** works by concentrating solar radiation onto a copper pipe positioned at the focal region of a parabolic reflector.

The heat absorbed by the copper pipe is transferred to the water flowing through it, increasing the water temperature.

---

## ☀️ Working Process

```text
Sunlight
   ↓
Parabolic Reflector
   ↓
Concentrated Solar Radiation
   ↓
Copper Pipe at Focal Region
   ↓
Heat Transfer to Water
   ↓
Heated Water
