# Solar Water Heater

A mechanical solar water heating system designed to heat water using solar energy and a reflective concentrator.

## Project Objective

The objective is to develop a simple, low-cost solar water heating system using a reflective surface to concentrate sunlight onto a copper pipe.

## Working Principle

Sunlight is reflected by the curved reflective surface and directed toward the copper pipe. Water flows through the copper pipe and gains heat from the solar energy.

## Key Features

- Mechanical-only system
- No sensors
- No electronics
- Copper pipe for water heating
- Reflective solar concentrator
- Low-cost construction
- Uses renewable solar energy

## Future Scope

The system can be improved by optimizing the reflector shape, pipe position, insulation, and water flow rate to improve heating performance.
