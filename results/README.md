# Results

## 📌 Overview

This folder contains the experimental testing results and thermal performance observations of the **Parabolic Solar Water Heater Using Copper Pipe**.

The prototype was tested under outdoor sunlight conditions by passing water through the copper pipe positioned at the focal region.

---

## 🧪 Testing Parameters

The following parameters were considered during testing:

- Time
- Weather condition
- Inlet water temperature
- Outlet water temperature
- Temperature difference (ΔT)
- Water quantity / flow
- Exposure duration
- Solar irradiance, when available
