# Experimental Results

## Testing Setup

The prototype was tested outdoors under direct sunlight.

The reflector was oriented toward the sun and water was passed through the copper pipe.

The following parameters were observed:

- Time
- Weather condition
- Inlet water temperature
- Outlet water temperature
- Temperature difference
- Water quantity/flow
- Exposure duration
- Solar irradiance when available

## Observation

The prototype showed a measurable increase in outlet water temperature during outdoor testing.

The repeated tests also recorded similar temperature rises under comparable conditions.

## Conclusion from Testing

The experimental results demonstrate the working of the parabolic solar water heater and its ability to heat water using concentrated solar radiation.
